## 1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this task is to test the shopping cart and verify that product prices, delivery charges and final totals are calculated correctly.

The tests focus on the Everyday heavyweight tee and check that the delivery fee is applied correctly depending on the quantity purchased.

The expected results are:

* One T-shirt costs €18.00, with €2.90 delivery, making a total of €20.90.
* Two T-shirts cost €36.00, with free delivery, making a total of €36.00.
  

## 2. SCOPE OF TESTING

The following functionalities are included:

* Adding the Everyday heavyweight tee to the shopping cart.
* Checking the product price.
* Checking the subtotal for one item.
* Verifying the €2.90 delivery charge for one item.
* Increasing the quantity to two items.
* Verifying the subtotal of €36.00.
* Checking that delivery becomes free when the subtotal reaches €36.00.
* Verifying the final checkout total.
